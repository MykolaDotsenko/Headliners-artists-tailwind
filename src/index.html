FAQ

Useful answers, without another interface

Is Headliners a real festival?

No. It is a fictional product and frontend engineering case study.

Does the ticket button charge anything?

No. There is no checkout, payment processor, or collection of payment data.

What happens when I submit the newsletter form?

The form demonstrates accessible client-side feedback only. The email value is neither transmitted nor stored.
